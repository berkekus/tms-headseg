# Decision Record Template

This template is not a decision. Copy into `decisions/`, assign a unique ID, and keep `decision_state: proposed` until the choice is actually accepted.

Include the question, constraints, considered options, chosen/proposed option, reason, consequences, linked evidence, and unresolved conditions. For an accepted decision, identify the actual acceptance evidence and date. If superseded, retain the old record and link both directions.

Add the new record to the decision index. Update current state only when direction actually changes. Do not infer an advisor sign-off from the appearance of a new source PDF.
